# Saved decision brief

A brief ties each assessment to its saved clock, confirmation timeframe and
condition, current versus waiting-price evidence, structural target,
invalidation, event rechecks and known costs/data limitations. It introduces no
new direction, price target, signal threshold or execution authority.

The before-cost equality boundary (target + invalidation)/2 is derived from the
existing policy that remaining reward exceeds risk. For a long the relevant
interval is above invalidation and below the boundary; for a short it is above
the boundary and below invalidation. Endpoints are excluded. This is a
geometric margin reference, not a recommended entry range, actual fill price,
probability, cost-adjusted breakeven or validated profitable setup. The condition
must still be observed and reevaluated at its actual price; a hypothetical
better waiting price cannot rescue an exhausted observed move.

Original evidence remains in the same saved record. The brief references the
confirmation, current-price review, planned-price review and opposition fields
rather than duplicating their full text. Calendar enrichment refreshes the brief
before row copies and validation. Validator recomputes the brief if present;
old frozen records without it retain their original interpretation.

Selection version 4 displays the brief in the default report. Complete technical
comparison version 3 retains every technical reason and references the same
compressed view for full economic events, values, source and credit. This avoids
repeating long economic blocks per candidate and exceeding the existing900k
export bound. The default report keeps its calendar/source rendering. Versions
1/2/3 selection and 1/2 comparison-context exports still reproduce their exact
original text on restoration. No canonical evidence is removed to fit a bound.

Local restoration and replay demonstrate consistency and usability of saved
information, not improved live performance or a substitute for current prices.
